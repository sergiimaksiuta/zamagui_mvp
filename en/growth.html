FAQ / ZAMAGUI

Frequently asked questions for this direction

Short answers about format, expectations and the first step.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Mostly small and mid-sized businesses where the owner needs more order in operations, team structure, sales or growth initiatives.

Can we start from just one block?

Yes. Many engagements start from diagnostics, sales or the owner model and then scale step by step.

What is the result of the first stage?

A structured list of priorities, a change map, an implementation format and clear criteria to track progress.

Do you offer long-term support?

Yes. If you need sustainable change, we can stay involved through implementation, control and regular work sessions.
