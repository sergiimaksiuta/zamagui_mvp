Is this suitable for a small team?

Yes. Small teams often feel the impact fastest because automation immediately reduces routine work.

Not necessarily. We start from what you already use: chats, CRM, sheets, forms, knowledge bases and documents.

What can be automated first?

Lead processing, request routing, standard replies, internal tasks, draft generation, reports and status tracking.

Do you train the team?

Yes. We help with both setup and adoption so the team can use AI responsibly and consistently.
