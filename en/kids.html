FAQ / ZAMAGUI

Frequently asked questions for this direction

Short answers about format, expectations and the first step.

Who are these programs for?

Our main focus is teenagers aged 12–16 who want to develop modern competencies, independence and practical thinking.

What is the difference between Elite and Talent?

Elite is a premium long-term path with deeper support. Talent is a strong-entry format for motivated participants, with support options.

Is there real practice, not just theory?

Yes. We focus on projects, communication, mini-sprints, real tasks, presentations and visible results.

How does admission work?

We begin with a short conversation with parents and the child to suggest the right format, level and learning trajectory.
