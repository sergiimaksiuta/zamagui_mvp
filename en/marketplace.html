FAQ / ZAMAGUI

Frequently asked questions for this direction

Short answers about format, expectations and the first step.

Who is Marketplace for?

For experts, service teams, masters and partners who want not just visibility, but clear and modern sales presentation.

Can I start with only one service?

Yes. A single well-packaged offer is often the best entry point and later becomes the base for scaling.

What matters more: design or the offer itself?

Both matter. We first clarify the value and the client journey, then support it with strong visual presentation.

Can partner sales be included?

Yes. Marketplace becomes stronger when your offer is connected with community-based referrals and partnerships.
