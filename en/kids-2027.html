05 / QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Does this secure a place?

No. Pre-registration records your interest. Enrolment and terms are agreed separately.

Is experience required?

Not necessarily. We discuss starting skills and adapt the learning path.

Who may submit the form?

A parent or legal guardian for ages 12–17; participants aged 18+ may submit themselves.

What does it cost?

Pricing and full terms will be provided before any agreement. There is no pre-registration charge.
