Frequently asked questions for this direction

Short answers about format, expectations and the first step.

Do you work only with investment projects?

No. Capital is also useful for internal launches, new business directions, partnership models and pilot preparation.

What if the idea is still very raw?

That is a common starting point. We help structure the idea, test the logic, risks and basic economics.

Can you help only with the financial model?

Yes. We can work on unit economics, a financial model, presentation materials or preparation for a partner meeting as separate blocks.

What result should I expect?

A structured project with logic, numbers, a roadmap and materials that support the next real decision.
