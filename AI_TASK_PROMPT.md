# ZaMaGui — AI task prompt for solution pages

Redesign all six solution pages (Growth, Kids, AI / IT, Guild, Marketplace, Capital) so they become more persuasive, interconnected and visually cleaner.

Requirements:
1. Make each page unique while preserving the shared design system.
2. Rebuild the solution cards so they look related but not repetitive, and each card is clearly tied to a relevant block, section or CTA.
3. Add page-specific FAQ blocks with relevant questions and useful answers.
4. Add several blog / insight / CTA blocks to support SEO, trust and conversion.
5. Remove generic placeholder phrases such as “Illustrative images...” and replace them with real, normal text.
6. Ensure expandable blocks change layout adaptively on desktop and mobile.
7. Keep visuals crisp, modern and personal: different people, strong eye contact or side glance, no awkward poses, no unclear faces.
8. Preserve three-language support (UA / EN / RU) and light / dark theme compatibility.
9. Make the pages feel connected to the whole ecosystem and commercially stronger.
10. Cards must be clickable and lead to relevant actions or deeper content.
